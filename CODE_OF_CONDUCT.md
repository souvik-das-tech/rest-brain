# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is
best for the community. Harassment, insults and personal or political attacks are not accepted, in
issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through the
[GitHub profile of souvik-das-tech](https://github.com/souvik-das-tech). All reports are reviewed
and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors, as
described in the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
